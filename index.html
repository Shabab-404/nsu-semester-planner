📖 How to Use This Planner

Follow these steps to generate your personalized graduation roadmap.

  1. Review Requirements: Check the Degree Requirements section to understand your target credits.
  2. Select Completed Courses: Click on the courses you have already passed in the main grid below to mark them with a checkmark.
  3. Log Electives: Scroll to the Specialized Electives section and tick any electives you have already finished.
  4. Add Retakes: If you failed a specific part (like just the lab), first check off the combined course in the grid above, then select your specific Theory/Lab retake in the Planner's dropdown.
  5. Generate & Edit Plan: Choose how many semesters you have left and click Generate. Drag & Drop courses between semesters to balance your credit load!
